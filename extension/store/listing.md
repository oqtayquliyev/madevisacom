## Description
Find jobs abroad before everyone else.

MadeVisa collects new jobs straight from company career pages — many of them never posted on LinkedIn or Indeed — and checks every one is still live. This extension brings them to you.

WHAT YOU GET
• New jobs, first — the newest jobs abroad in one click, with the company, country and a "Visa" mark when sponsorship is offered.
• Your own job alert — choose job titles or skills, a country and "visa sponsorship only". We check every 15 minutes to 3 hours and notify you when a new job matches.
• Search from anywhere — type a job and land on the matching jobs on MadeVisa.
• Every MadeVisa tool one click away — CV match (your score on every job), sponsorship documents for visa jobs, company insights (who replies, who sponsors) and visa routes by country.

FREE TO USE
The extension is free. A free MadeVisa account lets you apply to every job. The MadeVisa Membership ($12.50 once, lifetime access) adds CV tailoring on every job, unlimited sponsorship documents and more.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No access to your browsing or other websites. Your alert settings stay in your Chrome profile.

## Single purpose (Privacy tab)
Shows newly published jobs from MadeVisa and notifies the user about new jobs that match the job alert they set.

## Permission justifications (Privacy tab)
- alarms: to check for new matching jobs on the schedule the user picks (every 15 minutes to 3 hours).
- notifications: to tell the user when a new job matches their alert.
- storage: to keep the user's alert settings (job titles, country, visa-only, schedule) and which jobs they've already seen.
